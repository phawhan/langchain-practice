# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
All Acme-branded products include a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear.

## Extended Warranty
AcmeCare extends coverage to 3 years and adds accidental damage protection with two claims per year. AcmeCare must be purchased within 60 days of the original purchase. The deductible is $49 per accidental damage claim.

## Claims
To file a claim, provide the order number and a description of the issue. Defective items are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.

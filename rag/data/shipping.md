# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Options
Standard shipping takes 4-6 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $15. Same-day delivery is available in select metro areas for $25 when ordered before 11 AM.

## International Orders
We ship to 40 countries. International orders take 7-14 business days. Customers pay all import duties and taxes. Lithium batteries cannot be shipped internationally by air.

## Lost Packages
If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, contact support. We will open a carrier investigation and send a replacement or refund within 10 business days.

# Refund Policy

## Standard Refunds
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original packaging with all accessories included. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

## Opened Electronics
Opened laptops, phones, and tablets can be returned within 15 days. A 10% restocking fee applies unless the item is defective. Devices must be factory reset and unlocked from any accounts before return.

## Exceptions
This policy does not apply to gift cards, downloadable software, or clearance items marked Final Sale. These cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.
